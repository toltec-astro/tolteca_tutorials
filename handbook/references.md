# Instrument and software papers

These papers describe TolTEC's instrument development and earlier software
generations. Use them for the measurement principles and historical context. For
a reduction, also use the documentation for your installed software and the
calibration and quality information supplied with your data release.

| Topic                                           | Paper                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Instrument and laboratory characterization      | Wilson et al. (2020), [The TolTEC camera: an overview of the instrument and in-lab testing results](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2562331), SPIE 11453, 1145302                             |
| Optical design                                  | Lunde et al. (2020), [The optical design and performance of TolTEC: a millimeter-wave imaging polarimeter](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2562798), SPIE 11453, 114534A                      |
| Telescope alignment and optics characterization | Lunde et al. (2022), [The TolTEC camera: optical alignment and characterization](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630340), SPIE 12190, 1219016                                                |
| Half-wave plate and polarization measurement    | Lee et al. (2022), [The TolTEC camera: polarimetric commissioning and performance of the continuously rotating half-wave plate](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2627725), SPIE 12190, 121901O |
| Data reduction engine                           | McCrackan et al. (2022), [The TolTEC camera: the citlali data reduction pipeline engine](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629095), SPIE 12189, 121891H                                        |

This reading list is not a release-specific acknowledgement or citation policy.
Follow the instructions accompanying the data and software you use when
preparing a publication.
